I make electronic music that fits into several different genres. It's mostly under the downtempo umbrella, but there's also going to be some random stuff like breakcore or noise. I've finished one album, Sodium Lauryl Sulfate, which is available to listen to for free, or download. I have a bunch more in the works.

Some of my influences include Luxury Elite, Saint Pepsi, Chuck Person, Glimlip, mommy, Philanthrope, Idealism, Sleepy Fish, The Deli, Blank Banshee, Vektroid, and the work of Ellie Voyyd/Lick Nand over on cybergrunge.net. I have a lot more influences, too, but these are some of the most relevant ones.

(I'm not affiliated with any of these people, btw.)

I do not support the use of generative AI in the arts and go to great lengths to ensure it never touches any part of my process. No part of my music, visuals, or text is created or manipulated with any generative AI.
